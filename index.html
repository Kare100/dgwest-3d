    Sources & assumptions

    What the model takes from published material, and what it fills in. Sources sometimes disagree; both figures are shown where they do.

    • DG West brochure (Reportage Group) — developer brochure Floor plans for floors 5–24 and the 25th floor, unit areas (studio 41 m²; one-bed 65 + 5 m²; two-bed 85 + 5 m²), every room size, and the exterior renders used for the façade.

      Filled in by this model

    • Walls follow the printed room sizes; the brochure itself says its drawings are approximate and not to scale.
    • Storey heights aren't published: 3.2 m floor-to-floor and 2.75 m ceilings are assumed, putting the roof near 87 m.
    • Furniture follows the brochure plans; finishes, the neighbourhood and the orientation on the plot are approximations.
    • Prices aren't published, so none are shown.
